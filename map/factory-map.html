PM Factory

Six stages take an idea from "is this worth doing?" to "did it move the metric?". Pick your situation and the factory shows where to start. Open a stage to see its skills, what each one reads and writes, and the gate that decides when you move on.

How you move between stages

Menu of steps→Run steps→Exit checklist met→Gate: evidence→You decide→Next menu

Stages hand off through files. Each project has one STATE.md that names the exact files the next stage reads, so nothing scans the whole repo. You can skip ahead any time. The skip and its reason are logged.

Skills are original and written from first principles. Inspirations are listed in CREDITS.md.
